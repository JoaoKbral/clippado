Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

What is stored

  • OAuth tokens for the operator's own connected YouTube and TikTok accounts, plus the account's display name and id. They are stored only on the operator's computer and are used only to upload videos to those accounts.
  • Public live stream video and the public chat message count, used to choose and cut clips. Chat messages are not stored beyond a short rolling window.

What is not done

  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No data from other TikTok or YouTube users is collected.
  • This website has no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Deleting data

Revoke access from the platform's app settings (YouTube: Google Account → Security → Third-party access; TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps and services). Stored tokens are deleted on request.
