Clippado

Clippado is a personal tool that watches live streams, cuts short highlight clips of the moments where chat reacts the most, adds captions, and posts them to the operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Every post credits the streamer and links to the original channel. Streamers who want a clip removed, or want their channel excluded, can write to clippadooficial@gmail.com; requests are handled within 48 hours.

Clippado uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. See the Privacy Policy for what data it uses and the Terms of Service.

The tool only posts to accounts its operator owns and has explicitly connected.
